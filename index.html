Jungle Survival

Jungle mein kho gaye ho — 7 din zinda raho, bachaav dal aa raha hai!

Kaise khelein

Har din 3 kaam karo. Bhookh aur Pyaas ki bar khaali mat hone do. Paani aur khana dhoondhte raho. 2 lakri se aag, 4 se shelter banta hai. Aag ek raat chalegi; shelter hamesha rahega. Raat ko araam milega, par thand aur janwar se bachna hoga. Aage badhne se samaan ya jaldi bachaav mil sakta hai, par khatra bhi hai. Saatvin raat ke baad bachaav pakka hai!
